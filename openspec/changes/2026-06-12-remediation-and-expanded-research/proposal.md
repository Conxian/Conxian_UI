# Proposal: Remediation and Expanded Research (Updated July 2026)

## Goal
To expand research into cross-chain interoperability and remediate identified issues in the Linear backlog, ensuring the Conxian UI remains robust and aligned with institutional standards.

## Scope
- Context7 research on Bitcoin (sBTC), BitVM (BitVM2/3), and RGB (v0.11.1).
- Institutional Authentication research (Better Auth, Passkeys).
- Audit and remediation of Linear issues (CON-1402 Dependabot).
- UI/UX alignment with the Bright Foundation and naming standards.

## Approach
1. Use Context7 to gather deep technical insights into Bitcoin-native protocols.
2. Address technical debt and security vulnerabilities via dependency overrides.
3. Align UI nomenclature (NOMINAL -> READY) and visual standards (Ivory foundation).
4. Formalize the Documentation Alignment Index.
